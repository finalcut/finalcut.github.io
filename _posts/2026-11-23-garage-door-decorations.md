## Intro
This year I decided to buy a garage door decorative cover for our house for Halloween.  Our neighbors and I were doing a old-western style carnival and I wanted to get something that would help the garage blend in but while also having a young-kid friendly Halloween theme.  I found one at dbackdrop that really fit the bill. It's [fun friendly scene of hay, some jack-o-laterns, some cutout bats, and some sheet ghosts in front of an old barn door](https://www.dbackdrop.com/products/spooky-halloween-rustic-ghost-pumpkin-barn-garage-door-decor-csh67-33-2-car).

After ordering that I found a different garage door cover for Christmas - that features some big nutcrackers.  I got that one from Garagebuddy.  The halloween one I ordered with "Reinforced Grommes & Ropes" and the christmas one with "Velcro and Adjustable Elastic Straps"

## Comparison
Honestly, I don't think the quality of the two is much different from each other. The material is fairly thin cloth with a high quality image printed on them.  They both are the exact same size (18'W x 7' tall).  The biggest difference between them is in the installation method and speed of shipping

I had accidentally not ordered the halloween backdrop when I thought I had so I ordered it kind of last minute (about 2 weeks before Halloween) and then I started to worry that it wasn't going to arrive on time - it was pretty expensive since I got the large size (~$267) and I was going to be pretty bummed if it weren't installed for the Trick-or-Treaters.  Fortunately, it showed up on 12 days after I ordered it giving me a couple days of buffer.  I installed it right away and it was, quite frankly, an huge pain.

The Rope and Grommet method was no fun and quite difficult.  First just getting the top corner attached somewhere while also keeping the cover out of the installation seal on the side of the garage door was tough.  Secondly, the grommets were pulling the tarp apart in the corners so it looked like the earlobe of a person who had removed their guages.  I was pretty worried the whole thing would fall apart.  It was also hard to find sufficient places to tie down all the grommets with the rope where I wasn't also worried about influencing the operation of the garage door.

When all was said and done I got it installed but it was a bit offcenter and had a big wrinkle in it since my ropes weren't all perfectly the same length/tightness.

![Western Halloween Garage Door](https://rawlinson.us/assets/images/2026/halloween-garage-cover.png)

It looked fine but I don't think I can trust the installation method for a second year which is a bummer considering the amount of money it cost.

The Christmas cover, from Garage Buddy, features [four nutcrackers in front of some french doors and has some snowy christmas trees](https://garagebuddy.com/products/nutcracker-guards-christmas-garage-door-cover?variant=49082601603318).  It was also expensive but much less so than dbackdrop. I paid ~$144 for it.  It took a long time to get to me - about a month as it was shipped directly from China.  For this one I didn't get a choice for the grommets so I used the velcro and attached strap method.  It was VASTLY superior.  First you clean off near the top of the garage door (ehind the top weather strip).. Let it dry, then apply a LONG strand of velcro up there.  After that you let the velcro adhesive back cure for a day before adding the garage cover.  Adding the garage cover was pretty trivial - you have to have the garage door open just enough to get your arms and hands into the gap and you just start one one side (just outisde the weather seal) and work your way across.  I didn't have to fight with ropes or tearing fabric etc.  It installed flat and easy and has three bungies on the bottom that you loop under the garage door and hook onto metal parts of the garage door interior.  That's it.  I can use this one for a few years at least unless I do something dumb. 

![Christmas Nutcracker Garage Door](https://rawlinson.us/assets/images/2026/nutcracker-less-wrinkles.png)


I think had I gone with the velcro on the Halloween option it would be good to go for future years too but, I didn't and it isn't.  


In the end I really am happy with the GarageBuddy banner however, they have a much more limited set of choices and didn't have the perfect "country" theme type of cover I needed for our carnival.  Dbackdrop has a lot more options but is more expensive. In the end I'll probably shop from both stores again but I'll always opt for the velcro option. You should too.
